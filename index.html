Before you make the switch.

Will my baby take it?

Every baby is different, which is why Moka is covered by a 90-day guarantee. Try it through real feeds and send it back if it is not right.

I already found a bottle that works. Why switch?

You do not have to switch everything at once. Start with one bottle if you want glass milk contact, fewer parts, and a cleaner routine.

Does it work for the bottle-resistant?

Moka uses a wide, 15° naturally-shaped nipple to support a deeper latch, but no bottle can guarantee acceptance for every baby.

Is it really Swiss-engineered?

Yes. Moka is Swiss-engineered with a bottom vent, five laser-cut flow stages, and material choices designed around everyday feeding.

What's the nipple made of?

Platinum-cured silicone, chosen for softness, heat stability, and longer-lasting daily use than ordinary food-grade silicone.

Is it really lead-free?

Yes. Independent third-party testing confirmed Moka is lead-free.

How should I clean it to make it last?

Boil, steam, or UV when you need to. For longest life, hand-wash the silicone and PPSU parts and sterilize occasionally rather than every time.

How do I know which flow my baby needs?

Start by age, then watch the feed. Gulping or pulling off means slow it down; frustration or chewing means speed it up.

Will it fit my pump/warmer?

Pump compatibility depends on the pump and adapter. Moka is well-sized to fit most conventional pumps and warmers.

Does this prevent colic?

No bottle can prevent or cure colic. Moka is designed to help reduce swallowed air by keeping air out of the milk.

What is included?

Each bottle includes the bottle, nipple, collar, cap, and sealing disc. Replacement nipples are sold separately.

What's the guarantee?

Use Moka for 90 days through real feeds, washes, and nights. If it is not right for your baby, send it back for a refund.
